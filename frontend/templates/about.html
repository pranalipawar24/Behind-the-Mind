About Behind the Mind

Behind the Mind is an AI-powered mental health companion that combines a calm chat interface with an LLM-driven agent that can call tools: a therapeutic MedGemma-based responder, a location-aware therapist finder (Google Maps), and an emergency voice-call tool (Twilio).

What this app is

  • A safe, judgement-free space to talk.
  • A demo of tool-using AI agents for wellbeing support.
  • An integration showcase: FastAPI + LangGraph + Groq + Google Maps + Twilio.

What this app is not

  • Not a substitute for professional mental-health care.
  • Not a crisis or emergency service.
  • Not a diagnostic tool.

If you are in immediate danger

Please contact your local emergency number or a crisis hotline in your region right away.
